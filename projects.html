Featured projects

This page features a curated selection of projects I’ve worked on in recent years.

You’ll find UX/UI work created for MosaiQ Labs—a tech start-up leveraging AI to simplify complex workflows—alongside projects for heritage and culture charities.

I’ve also included a few front-end development projects I’ve taken on while learning HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React, including a fully functioning weather desktop app.

Together, these projects reflect my interest in designing thoughtful, human-centred digital systems—particularly where technology, complexity, and real-world decision-making intersect.

Flagship case study

MosaiQ Labs

Integrating AI into workflows

2023 - 2025

Junior UX/UI Designer · AI-driven B2B platform · End-to-end product design

My work at MosaiQ Labs focused on designing human–AI collaboration within a research platform. Across these case studies, I explored how interaction patterns, design systems, and AI-driven features reduce complexity, support real workflows, and make advanced technology feel understandable, reliable, and human.

The Old Tower Project

Re-vamping the charity's website -

December 2022 - February 2023

UX/UI Designer · Charity website · Research-led redesign

This redesign explores how a local organisation’s website can better reflect its community—improving clarity, navigation, and accessibility while preserving its identity. The project centred on understanding real user needs and translating them into an experience that feels welcoming, trustworthy, and easy to navigate.

Hotel Project

Designing a hotel booking system -

October 2022 - November 2022

UX/UI Designer · Booking flow & service design · Concept project

A graduation case study exploring a complete end-to-end UX process for a hotel booking system. I conducted user research, developed personas, mapped journeys, ideated and prototyped solutions, and iteratively tested the design to create an intuitive, brand-aligned booking experience that balances efficiency with a welcoming, human-centered interface.

This portfolio was designed and coded by Mor Shmueli. It is open-sourced and hosted on Netlify
